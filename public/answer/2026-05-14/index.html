📖 Meaning easy

丰富多彩 (fēng fù duō cǎi)

Rich and varied; abundant and colorful.

Literal: abundant, rich, many, colorful

💡 Example

The festival offers a rich and varied program of activities.

🎯 Hints (for next time)

Hint 1 — Subtle

Think of something that is plentiful and diverse.

Hint 2 — More Obvious

One character means 'many' or 'abundant'.

The idiom 丰富多彩 (fēng fù duō cǎi) is a widely-used Chinese expression meaning Rich and varied; abundant and colorful.. Literally, each character means: abundant, rich, many, colorful.
