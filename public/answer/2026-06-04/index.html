📖 Meaning medium

下落不明 (xià luò bù míng)

The whereabouts of someone or something are unknown or have not been clarified.

Literal: down fall not clear

💡 Example

After the accident, the missing hiker's whereabouts remain unknown.

🎯 Hints (for next time)

Hint 1 — Subtle

This idiom is about something or someone that has disappeared without a trace.

Hint 2 — More Obvious

The last character means 'bright' or 'clear'.
