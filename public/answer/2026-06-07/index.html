📖 Meaning medium

络绎不绝 (luò yì bù jué)

Describes a continuous, unending stream of people or vehicles coming and going.

Literal: incessant unbroken continuous

💡 Example

During the festival, visitors flocked to the temple, coming and going in an endless stream.

🎯 Hints (for next time)

Hint 1 — Subtle

Think of a busy street or a popular attraction where people keep passing by.

Hint 2 — More Obvious

One character means 'not', another means 'break' or 'end'.
