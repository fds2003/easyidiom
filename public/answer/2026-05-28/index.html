📖 Meaning medium

深入人心 (shēn rù rén xīn)

To become deeply rooted in people's hearts; to be widely accepted and influential.

Literal: deep enter people heart

💡 Example

The concept of environmental protection has gradually penetrated deep into the hearts of the people.

🎯 Hints (for next time)

Hint 1 — Subtle

This idiom describes an idea or feeling that has strong influence and is widely embraced.

Hint 2 — More Obvious

The third character means 'person' or 'people'.
