📖 Meaning easy

一年一度 (yī nián yī dù)

Once a year; annual.

Literal: one year one time

💡 Example

We celebrate Christmas once a year.

🎯 Hints (for next time)

Hint 1 — Subtle

This idiom describes something that happens every twelve months.

Hint 2 — More Obvious

Think about a calendar event that occurs once every cycle.
