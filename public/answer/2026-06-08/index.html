📖 Meaning medium

弄虚作假 (nòng xū zuò jiǎ)

To engage in fraud or deception; to fabricate or falsify something.

Literal: 弄 (make/do) 虚 (empty/false) 作 (make/do) 假 (fake)

💡 Example

The company was caught弄虚作假 in its financial reports.

🎯 Hints (for next time)

Hint 1 — Subtle

This idiom describes dishonest behavior to deceive others.

Hint 2 — More Obvious

The second character means 'empty' or 'unreal'.
