📖 Meaning medium

名不虚传 (míng bù xū chuán)

Reputation is not false; one lives up to one's name or fame.

Literal: name not false spread

💡 Example

After tasting the famous dish, I had to agree it was truly delicious—the reputation was not false.

🎯 Hints (for next time)

Hint 1 — Subtle

This idiom is about verifying that a good reputation is deserved.

Hint 2 — More Obvious

The second character means 'not', and the third character means 'empty' or 'false'.
