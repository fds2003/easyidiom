📖 Meaning medium

如火如荼 (rú huǒ rú tú)

Describes something that is growing vigorously and irresistibly, like a prairie fire.

Literal: like fire like flowering reed

💡 Example

The movement spread like wildfire, growing with ru huo ru tu momentum.

🎯 Hints (for next time)

Hint 1 — Subtle

Think of something spreading intensely, with passion and unstoppable force.

Hint 2 — More Obvious

It involves two natural elements that are often associated with growth and destruction.
