Romantic Chinese Idioms About Love and Relationships

Love has been a central theme in Chinese literature for thousands of years. Chinese idioms offer a deeply poetic, aesthetic way to describe devotion, destiny, and eternal relationships. Whether you want to impress your partner or study Mandarin romantic literature, these idioms are essential.
海枯石烂
hǎi kū shí làn

Chinese Definition: 海水干涸、石头腐烂。形容历时久远。比喻坚定的意志永远不变。

Derivation: 宋·王奕《法曲献仙音·和朱静翁青溪词》老我重来，海干石烂，那复断碑残础。”

Example Sentence: 汝要说我降，除非～。★明·罗贯中《三国演义》第四十七回

一心一意
yī xīn yī yì

English Meaning: To be completely devoted or focused on a single task or person.

Chinese Definition: 只有一个心眼儿，没有别的考虑。

Derivation: 《三国志·魏志·杜恕传》免为庶人，徙章武郡，是岁嘉平元年。”裴松之注引《杜氏新书》故推一心，任一意，直而行之耳。”

Example Sentence: She studied for the exam with single-minded dedication, ignoring all distractions.

一见钟情
yī jiàn zhōng qíng

English Meaning: Love at first sight.

Chinese Definition: 钟集中；钟情爱情专注。旧指男女之间一见面就产生爱情。也指对事物一见就产生了感情。

Derivation: 清·古吴墨浪子《西湖佳话·西泠韵迹》乃蒙郎君一见钟情，故贱妾有感于心。”

Example Sentence: They met at a party and it was love at first sight.
